# Is this Ukrainian rule taken from the EU text? (provenance protocol, Ukrainian Methodology, v1)

You read proposition U from a Methodology of the Antimonopoly Committee of
Ukraine for defining a market and establishing a monopoly (dominant) position
-- the 2002 text (z0317-02) or its 2026 successor (z1043-26) -- in Ukrainian,
and paragraph E from a notice of the European Commission, in English. You
decide whether U comes from E. You do not judge whether either rule is right,
or whether the Ukrainian authority should have followed the EU.

## The three answers

**rendered** — U renders E, or a part of E, into Ukrainian: the same rule
with the same particulars -- the same criteria, conditions, thresholds,
periods, examples or list items, mostly in the same order -- so that U reads
as a translation or a close paraphrase of E. U may leave out part of E, or
E's examples, and still be **rendered**, as long as what U does say follows E
point by point.

**same rule** — U and E state the same rule, but U is not a rendering of E:
U words it its own way, with its own particulars (other criteria, another
threshold or period, another list), or states only the general idea E spells
out.

**different** — U and E are about different rules, or share only the topic
or general vocabulary of competition law (market, competitor, substitution,
price).

## Rules for hard cases

- Generic statements every competition-law text makes ("the market is
  defined from the demand side", "market shares indicate market power") are
  **same rule** at most: say **rendered** only when the particulars match.
- A definition in U and a paragraph of E that uses the same concept without
  defining it: **different**.
- A list in U that reproduces E's list item by item, even shortened:
  **rendered**. A list that shares one or two items with E's: **same rule**.
- A threshold, a period or a percentage that differs between U and E
  ("two years" against "one year", "5 to 10 %" against "5 %"): not
  **rendered**; **same rule** if the rule is otherwise E's.
- E is about the Commission's own procedure (its notices, its cases, its
  courts): U can only be **same rule** if it states the same rule for the
  Ukrainian authority.

## Answer

Return only JSON: {"answer": "rendered" | "same rule" | "different", "why": "<one sentence>"}
