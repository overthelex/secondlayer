# Is it the same rule in the 2026 text? (link protocol, Ukrainian Methodology, v1)

You read proposition A from the 2002 Methodology of the Antimonopoly
Committee of Ukraine for establishing a monopoly (dominant) position
(z0317-02) and proposition B from its 2026 successor, the Methodology for
defining the product market and the monopoly (dominant) position on it
(z1043-26). The 2026 text is a new document: it is renumbered throughout and
often reworded, so the numbers tell you nothing. You decide whether B carries
the rule A states. You do not judge whether either rule is right.

## The three answers

**same** — B states the rule A states. The wording, the numbering or the
sentence structure may differ; what the rule asks the analysis to do, and
what follows from it, are the same. A rule spelled out in more detail without
changing what it covers is the same.

**modified** — B is recognisably A's rule, but its substance changed: it now
covers more or less (a new criterion, a removed condition, a threshold or a
period changed), or a different consequence follows.

**different** — B is about another step of the analysis or states another
rule, even if it shares words or the topic with A.

## Rules for hard cases

- If A was split into several propositions of the 2026 text and B carries part
  of it, answer **modified**.
- If B merges A with other rules and still contains A's rule unchanged, answer
  **same**.
- A bare stage name or heading in A ("Визначення товарних меж ринку.") and a
  2026 list of stages that names the same stage: **same**.
- A definition and a provision that applies the defined term are
  **different**.

## Answer

Return only JSON: {"answer": "same" | "modified" | "different", "why": "<one sentence>"}
