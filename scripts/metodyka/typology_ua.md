# What kind of statement is this proposition? (typology protocol, Ukrainian Methodology, v1)

You read one proposition of the Methodology of the Antimonopoly Committee of
Ukraine (AMCU) for defining a market and establishing a monopoly (dominant)
position -- either the 2002 text (z0317-02) or its 2026 successor (z1043-26).
The text is in Ukrainian. You decide what the proposition does in the text.
You do not judge whether it is right, applied, or important.

## The five types

**housekeeping** — says something about the text itself, not about markets or
undertakings: its legal basis, its adoption, its entry into force, whom it
binds, how it is structured, or a bare reference to another document or to
the statute's own definitions.

**scope** — says in which proceedings or for which purposes the Methodology
is used, what it covers or does not cover, or how it relates to other acts.

**definition** — says what a term means (for example product market,
interchangeability, target product, potential competitor, barriers to entry),
without itself stating how a market is defined or dominance is established.

**rationale** — explains why: the purpose of a step, the economic reasoning
behind it. It gives no criterion a decision could apply to a case.

**norm** — states how the analysis is done or concluded: a criterion, a
method, a threshold, a presumption, an indicator and how to read it, a step
the AMCU must take, a source it must or may use, a consequence. Something a
decision can apply to the facts of a case.

## Order of the questions

Ask them in this order and stop at the first yes.

1. Is it about the text itself (basis, adoption, force, binding effect,
   structure, bare reference)? → housekeeping
2. Does it say in which proceedings the Methodology is used, or what it
   covers or excludes? → scope
3. Does it say what a term means, and nothing about how the analysis is
   done? → definition
4. Does it only explain why, without a criterion? → rationale
5. Otherwise → norm

## Rules for hard cases

- A bare stage name or heading-like item ("Визначення товарних меж ринку.")
  that asserts nothing is **housekeeping**: it structures the text.
- A list of the stages of the analysis that tells the AMCU what to do, in
  what order, is a **norm**.
- A definition that also states how the analysis is done ("... are
  interchangeable if ... and the AMCU treats them as one market") is a
  **norm**. A definition that only classifies is a **definition**.
- A provision that restates a threshold of the statute (Article 12 of the Law
  on the Protection of Economic Competition) is typed by what it says: a
  threshold the analysis applies is a **norm**, even if the statute already
  contains it.
- A list of information sources the AMCU uses is a **norm**.

## Answer

Return only JSON: {"type": "<one of the five>", "why": "<one sentence>"}
