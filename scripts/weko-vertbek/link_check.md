# Is it the same rule in the next version? (link protocol, v1)

You read proposition A from one version of the Swiss Competition Commission's
notice on vertical agreements (Vertikalbekanntmachung) or its explanatory
notes (Erläuterungen), and proposition B from the next version. You decide
whether B carries the rule A states. You do not judge whether either rule is
right.

## The three answers

**same** — B states the rule A states. The wording, the numbering, the
gendered forms or the sentence structure may differ; the conduct the rule is
about and what follows for it are the same. A rule spelled out in more detail
without changing what it covers is the same.

**modified** — B is recognisably A's rule, but its substance changed: it now
covers more or less conduct (a new exception, a removed condition, a
threshold changed), or a different consequence follows (for example, treated
as severe instead of assessed case by case).

**different** — B is about other conduct or states another rule, even if it
shares words, the topic or the place in the text with A.

## Rules for hard cases

- A lettered point is read together with the opening of its sentence, which is
  given to you when there is one.
- If A was split into several propositions of the next version and B carries
  part of it, answer **modified**.
- If B merges A with another rule and still contains A's rule unchanged,
  answer **same**.
- A recital that summarises an operative provision and an operative provision
  are **different**, even when they say the same thing.

## Answer

Return only JSON: {"answer": "same" | "modified" | "different", "why": "<one sentence>"}
