## 1. General Provisions

1.1. These Legal Consultation Marketplace Rules (hereinafter — "Rules") define the procedure for interaction between Clients and Attorneys on the LEX AI platform (hereinafter — "Platform", "Marketplace") during the creation, execution, and completion of Orders for legal consultations.

1.2. These Rules are an integral part of the Public Offer for the provision of information services using the LEX AI service (hereinafter — "Main Offer") and the Public Offer for Attorneys on the provision of legal consultation marketplace services (hereinafter — "Attorney Offer").

1.3. By accepting the Main Offer (for Clients) or the Attorney Offer (for Attorneys), the parties automatically accept these Rules in their entirety.

1.4. These Rules shall have legal force in accordance with Articles 633, 641, and 642 of the Civil Code of Ukraine and constitute a special document detailing the procedure for the provision of services through the Marketplace. In the event of any conflict between these Rules and the offers, the provisions of these Rules shall prevail with respect to the Order execution process.

1.5. The Platform acts solely as an intermediary providing technical infrastructure for connecting Clients with Attorneys and is not a party to the legal relationship between them.

## 2. Definitions

Terms not defined in these Rules shall have the meanings established by the Main Offer and the Attorney Offer. In addition to the previously defined terms, the following terms are used in these Rules:

**"Order"** — a request submitted by the Client through the Platform to receive a legal Consultation from a specific Attorney, specifying the subject, type of service, and agreed price.

**"Consultation"** — a legal service provided by the Attorney to the Client through the Platform (text, video, or audio consultation) in the course of fulfilling an Order.

**"Escrow"** — a mechanism whereby the Platform holds funds in a special account until proper completion of the Order is confirmed or conditions for automatic payout are met.

**"Complaint"** — a formal statement by the Client or Attorney regarding improper performance of obligations by the other party, submitted through the Platform interface.

**"Rating"** — a numerical score on a scale of 1 to 5 stars assigned by the Client to the Attorney upon completion of an Order.

**"Review"** — a text comment by the Client regarding the quality of the Consultation provided, published together with the Rating.

**"Case Materials"** — documents, files, and other information transmitted by the Client to the Attorney through the Platform for the purposes of providing the Consultation.

**"Order Status"** — the current stage of Order execution (created, paid, in progress, completed, rated, closed, archived).

## 3. Order Creation and Execution Process

An Order proceeds through the following stages:

**3.1. Request Initiation by the Client.**
The Client creates a Consultation request specifying: the subject and description of the legal question, the preferred type of Consultation (text, video, audio), and the preferred date and time (if applicable). The request is saved in the system with the status "created."

**3.2. Attorney Search and Selection.**
The Client selects an Attorney through the Platform catalog based on specialization, Rating, reviews, and Attorney Rates, or receives a proposal from an Attorney who responded to the request.

**3.3. Order Confirmation.**
Both parties confirm the Order terms: scope of the Consultation, price, and timeline. Upon confirmation, the Order receives the status "confirmed."

**3.4. Payment and Escrow.**
The Client makes payment in accordance with the agreed price. Funds are credited to the Platform's escrow account and held until proper completion of the Order. The Order receives the status "paid."

**3.5. Consultation Delivery.**
The Attorney provides the Consultation in accordance with the agreed terms through the Platform's communication tools. The Order has the status "in progress." The Attorney is required to provide the first response within 24 (twenty-four) hours of payment.

**3.6. Completion Confirmation.**
After providing the Consultation, the Attorney marks the Order as completed. The Client confirms completion or files a Complaint. If the Client takes no action within 7 (seven) calendar days of the Attorney marking the Order as completed, completion shall be deemed automatically confirmed.

**3.7. Rating and Review.**
After confirmation of completion, the Client may leave a Rating (from 1 to 5 stars) and a text Review. Rating is voluntary but recommended.

**3.8. Payment to the Attorney.**
After confirmation of completion (explicit or automatic), the Platform initiates payment to the Attorney minus the Commission in accordance with the terms of the Attorney Offer. Payment is made within 5 (five) business days to the Attorney's bank details.

**3.9. Order Closure.**
The Order receives the status "closed" after payment is made and the Complaint filing period has expired.

**3.10. Archival.**
Closed Orders are automatically archived after 30 (thirty) calendar days. Archived Orders remain accessible for viewing by both parties but cannot be modified.

## 4. Communication Rules

4.1. All communication between the Client and the Attorney within the scope of an Order shall be conducted exclusively through the Platform's communication tools (chat, video calls, audio calls).

4.2. The parties are prohibited from exchanging personal contact information (phone numbers, personal email addresses, messengers) for the purpose of continuing cooperation outside the Platform to avoid paying the Commission, in accordance with Section 8 of the Attorney Offer.

4.3. The Platform reserves the right to moderate communications for the purpose of:
- ensuring compliance with these Rules;
- preventing fraud and abuse;
- resolving Complaints and disputes.

4.4. The Platform does not have access to the content of Consultations conducted through secure communication channels (video and audio consultations with end-to-end encryption), except where one of the parties provides a recording as part of the Complaint review procedure.

4.5. The parties shall maintain a polite and professional manner of communication. Insults, threats, discrimination, and other forms of unacceptable behavior are prohibited.

## 5. Case Materials Transfer Rules

5.1. The Client independently determines the scope of Case Materials to be transferred to the Attorney through the Platform for the purposes of the Consultation.

5.2. Transfer of Case Materials shall be conducted exclusively through the Platform's secure storage. The Client is responsible for the legality and accuracy of the transferred materials.

5.3. The Attorney's access to Case Materials is automatically revoked 7 (seven) calendar days after the Order is closed, unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

5.4. The Attorney undertakes to:
- use Case Materials exclusively for the purposes of providing the Consultation;
- not copy, distribute, or transfer Materials to third parties;
- comply with attorney-client privilege requirements in accordance with Article 22 of the Law of Ukraine "On the Bar and Practice of Law."

5.5. The Platform ensures technical protection of Case Materials (encryption in transit and at rest) but is not responsible for the content of transferred documents. If the Client has activated end-to-end encryption (E2EE), the encryption key for each document is cryptographically re-encrypted for the Attorney when transferring Materials. The Attorney's access to encrypted Materials is automatically revoked upon completion or cancellation of the Order.

## 6. Escrow and Payments

6.1. Funds paid by the Client for an Order are held in the Platform's escrow account until grounds for payout arise.

6.2. Grounds for payment to the Attorney:
- explicit confirmation of Order completion by the Client; or
- automatic confirmation of completion 7 (seven) calendar days after the Attorney marks the Order as completed (in the absence of Complaints).

6.3. The amount paid to the Attorney equals the Order price minus the Platform Commission as determined in accordance with Section 5 of the Attorney Offer:

| | Informational Services | Representation Services |
|---|---|---|
| **Attorney's Client** (invitation) | 10% | 10% |
| **Marketplace Client** (found independently) | 60% | 10% |

6.4. The minimum payout amount is 100 (one hundred) Ukrainian hryvnias. If the accumulated amount is less than the minimum, the payout is carried over to the next settlement period.

6.5. Payments are made to the bank details specified by the Attorney in the Profile within 5 (five) business days from the date grounds for payment arise.

6.6. In the event of a Complaint filed by the Client, payment is withheld pending resolution of the Complaint in accordance with Section 7 of these Rules, but for no longer than 30 (thirty) calendar days.

## 7. Complaints and Refunds

**7.1. Grounds for Filing a Complaint by the Client:**
- failure to provide the Consultation within the agreed timeframe;
- material discrepancy between the Consultation provided and the agreed scope;
- provision of knowingly incorrect legal information;
- violation of the Communication Rules by the Attorney (Section 4);
- other material breaches of the Order terms.

**7.2. Grounds for Filing a Complaint by the Attorney:**
- failure by the Client to provide necessary Materials for the Consultation;
- wrongful refusal by the Client to confirm completion;
- violation of the Communication Rules by the Client (Section 4).

**7.3. Complaint Review Procedure:**

7.3.1. A Complaint shall be filed through the Platform interface within 5 (five) calendar days from the date the grounds arose.

7.3.2. The Platform shall review the Complaint within 5 (five) business days from the date of receipt, with the right to request additional explanations and evidence from both parties.

7.3.3. Based on the review, the Platform shall render one of the following decisions:
- **Full Refund** — return of 100% of the Order price to the Client (in the event of failure to provide the Consultation or material violations by the Attorney);
- **Partial Refund** — return of a portion of the Order price to the Client with payment of the remainder to the Attorney (in the event of partial performance);
- **Refund Denial** — payment of the full amount to the Attorney (in the event of proper performance and the Complaint being unfounded).

7.3.4. Refund to the Client shall be made within 10 (ten) business days from the date of the decision to the payment details from which payment was made.

7.3.5. The Platform's decision on a Complaint may be appealed in accordance with the dispute resolution procedure set forth in Section 13 of the Main Offer.

## 8. Ratings and Reviews

8.1. After completion of an Order, the Client may rate the Attorney on a scale of 1 to 5 stars and leave a text Review.

8.2. The Attorney's Rating on the Platform is calculated based on the average of all ratings over the preceding 12 (twelve) months.

8.3. Review Moderation. The Platform reserves the right to remove a Review in the following cases:
- it contains insults, threats, or discrimination;
- it contains personal data of third parties;
- it is unrelated to the Consultation provided;
- it is knowingly false or manipulative.

8.4. The Attorney has the right to post a public response to a Review through the Platform interface.

8.5. **Minimum Rating.** If the Attorney's average Rating drops below 3.0 stars based on at least 5 (five) rated Orders, the Platform may:
- temporarily restrict the visibility of the Attorney's Profile in the catalog;
- send recommendations to the Attorney regarding service quality improvement;
- in the event of a persistently low Rating (below 3.0 for 3 consecutive months) — suspend the Attorney's Profile in accordance with Section 4.2 of the Attorney Offer.

8.6. Reviews and Ratings are the property of the Platform and shall not be removed upon termination of the Agreement with the Attorney.

9.1. The Client's liability is determined by the Main Offer; the Attorney's liability is determined by the Attorney Offer. These Rules supplement but do not replace the provisions of the aforementioned documents.

9.2. The Platform shall not be liable for:
- the content, quality, and results of legal Consultations;
- decisions made by the Client based on the Consultation;
- actions or omissions of the Attorney or Client;
- losses arising from the parties' violation of these Rules;
- the accuracy and completeness of Case Materials transferred by the Client.

9.3. The Platform is liable solely for the proper functioning of the Marketplace's technical infrastructure, including the escrow system and communication tools.

9.4. The Platform's aggregate liability is limited in accordance with the provisions of the Main Offer and the Attorney Offer.
