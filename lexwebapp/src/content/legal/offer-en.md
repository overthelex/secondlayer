## 2. Definitions

**"Service"** or **"LEX AI"** — software available at legal.org.ua, including APIs, MCP servers, and the web application, providing AI analysis of legal documents, semantic search, and related functions.

**"Services"** — information services provided by the Provider to the Client using the Service, as listed in Section 3 of this Agreement.

**"Pricing Plan"** — a set of conditions for the provision of Services (scope, cost, limitations) published on the Service website.

**"Account"** — the Client's personal section in the Service, created during registration.

**"Content"** — any data, documents, queries, and analysis results created or uploaded by the Client.

**"AI Analysis"** — automated processing of legal documents using artificial intelligence technologies.

**"MCP Token"** — an access key for connecting external MCP clients (Claude Desktop, etc.).

**"Reporting Period"** — a calendar month during which Services are provided.

**"Marketplace"** — a section of the Service that facilitates interaction between Clients and Attorneys for the provision of legal consultations through the Platform.

**"Attorney"** — a natural person holding a valid License to practice law who has been verified on the Platform in accordance with the Public Offer for Attorneys.

**"Consultation"** — a legal service provided by the Attorney to the Client through the Platform (text, video, or audio consultation).

**"Order"** — a request submitted by the Client through the Platform to receive a legal Consultation from an Attorney.

**"Escrow"** — a mechanism whereby the Provider holds funds in a special account until proper completion of the Order is confirmed.

**"Platform Commission"** — a fee for the Provider's information services, retained from each transaction in accordance with the Public Offer for Attorneys.

**"Marketplace Rules"** — a separate document defining the procedure for the creation, execution, and completion of Orders, communication rules, escrow mechanism, complaints, and ratings. The Marketplace Rules are an integral part of this Agreement.
