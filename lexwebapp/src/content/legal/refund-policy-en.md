Refunds may be issued in the following cases:

- erroneous or duplicate payment;
- technical inability to provide access to the service due to the Company's fault;
- cancellation of the service before its actual provision.

## 3. Refund Request Procedure

To request a refund, the client must submit a written request to: **info@legal.org.ua**

The request must include:

- full name or company name;
- payment date;
- payment amount;
- reason for refund;
- bank details for the refund.

The request is reviewed within **10 business days**.

If the refund is approved, the funds are returned using the same payment method within **5–10 banking days**.

- if the service has already been provided;
- if the client has violated the terms of use;
- if the request is submitted more than **14 calendar days** after the payment date (unless otherwise stipulated in the agreement).
