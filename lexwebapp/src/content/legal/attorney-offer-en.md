1.1. This document constitutes an official public proposal (offer) of Individual Entrepreneur Kyrychenko Igor Viktorovych (hereinafter — "Platform", "Company") to enter into an Agreement on the provision of legal consultation marketplace services (hereinafter — "Agreement") under the terms set forth below, with an attorney duly registered in accordance with applicable law (hereinafter — "Attorney").

1.3. Acceptance of this offer is effected by clicking the "Accept Offer" button in the Platform interface upon first access to the Attorney Profile.

1.5. This Agreement is supplementary to the main Public Offer for the provision of information services using the LEX AI service and governs relations arising in connection with the Attorney providing legal consultations through the Platform.

## 2. Definitions

**"Platform"** or **"LEX AI"** — software available at legal.org.ua that provides a marketplace connecting Clients with Attorneys, AI tools for legal analysis, communication infrastructure, and payment processing.

**"Attorney Profile"** — the Attorney's public page on the Platform containing information about specialization, experience, rates, reviews, and contact details.

**"Client"** — a natural or legal person seeking legal services through the Platform.

**"Consultation"** — a legal service provided by the Attorney to the Client through the Platform (text, video, or audio consultation).

**"Informational Services"** — consultations, legal analysis, document preparation, legal opinions, and other services that do not require representation in court or other bodies.

**"Representation Services"** — representation of the Client's interests in courts, government agencies, before third parties, and other services beyond the scope of informational services.

**"Attorney's Client"** — a Client brought to the Platform by the Attorney's invitation (referral link, direct invitation code).

**"Marketplace Client"** — a Client who independently found and selected the Attorney through the Platform's search or catalog.

**"Attorney Rating"** — a numerical score from 0 to 100, calculated monthly based on Client reviews over the preceding 12 (twelve) months of work on the Platform.

**"Attorney Rates"** — the cost of services independently set by the Attorney in their Profile.

**"Commission"** — the fee retained by the Platform from each transaction for marketplace services provided, the amount of which is determined in accordance with Section 5 of this Agreement.

**"License"** — the License to practice law, issued in accordance with the Law of Ukraine "On the Bar and Practice of Law."

**"URAU"** — the Unified Register of Attorneys of Ukraine, maintained by the Ukrainian National Bar Association.

## 3. Subject of the Agreement

3.1. The Platform undertakes to provide the Attorney with:
- access to the legal consultation marketplace for attracting Clients;
- AI tools for consultation preparation, document analysis, and case law research;
- communication infrastructure (chat, video calls);
- payment acceptance and processing system;
- analytical tools and statistics.

3.2. The Attorney undertakes to provide quality legal consultations to Clients in accordance with applicable law, the Rules of Attorney Ethics, and the terms of this Agreement.

3.3. The Platform is **NOT** a party to the legal relationship between the Attorney and the Client and shall not be liable for the content, quality, or results of legal consultations.

## 4. Verification and Admission

4.1. To obtain Attorney status on the Platform, the following is required:
- a valid License to practice law;
- active registration in the URAU;
- verification by the Platform (data verification in the URAU).

4.2. The Platform reserves the right to refuse verification or to suspend/block the Attorney Profile in the following cases:
- provision of false information;
- suspension or revocation of the License;
- removal from the URAU;
- systematic Client complaints;
- violation of the terms of this Agreement or the Rules of Attorney Ethics;
- other grounds provided by law.

4.3. The Attorney undertakes to immediately notify the Platform of any changes in the status of the License or URAU registration.

## 5. Commission and Payments

**5.1. Commission Rates:**

The Platform's Commission depends on the source of Client acquisition and the type of services:

| | Informational Services | Representation Services |
|---|---|---|
| **Attorney's Client** (invitation) | 10% | 10% |
| **Marketplace Client** (found independently) | 60% | 10% |

5.2. **Commission Reduction for High Rating.** For Marketplace Clients, the Commission on informational services is gradually reduced from 60% to 30% over 12 (twelve) months in equal installments, provided the Attorney maintains a positive Rating (at least 80 out of 100 points):
- The reduction is 2.5 percentage points per month (30% ÷ 12 months);
- Reduction schedule: Month 1 — 57.5%, Month 2 — 55%, Month 3 — 52.5%, ..., Month 12 — 30%;
- The reduction commences from the first full calendar month in which the Attorney's Rating reaches or exceeds 80 points.

Compliance verification is performed automatically on a monthly basis. If the Rating drops below 80 points, the gradual Commission reduction is suspended until the Rating is restored. If the Rating remains below 80 points for 3 (three) consecutive months, the Commission reverts to the base rate of 60% from the following calendar month, and the gradual reduction schedule restarts from the beginning.

5.3. **Payment Procedure.** Payment to the Attorney shall be made after confirmation of Consultation completion by both parties (Attorney and Client) or automatically 72 (seventy-two) hours after Consultation completion in the absence of complaints.

5.4. Payments shall be made to the bank details specified by the Attorney in the Profile within 5 (five) business days.

5.5. The minimum payout amount is 100 (one hundred) Ukrainian hryvnias. If the accumulated amount is less than the minimum, the payout is carried over to the next settlement period.

5.6. In the event of a substantiated complaint by the Client, the Platform reserves the right to withhold payment until the complaint is resolved, but for no longer than 30 (thirty) calendar days.

5.7. The Attorney is solely responsible for payment of taxes and fees on income received through the Platform in accordance with applicable law.

## 6. Rights and Obligations of the Attorney

**6.1. The Attorney undertakes to:**
- provide Consultations in accordance with the requirements of the Law of Ukraine "On the Bar and Practice of Law" and the Rules of Attorney Ethics;
- respond to Client inquiries within 24 hours (first response);
- keep Profile information current;
- refrain from providing knowingly incorrect legal consultations;
- maintain confidentiality in accordance with attorney-client privilege (Article 22 of the Law);
- notify the Client of any conflict of interest (if applicable);
- use the Platform in good faith and refrain from actions that harm the Platform's reputation.

**6.2. The Attorney has the right to:**
- independently set Rates for services;
- choose specialization and case categories;
- refuse to provide a Consultation for justified reasons;
- use the Platform's AI tools for Consultation preparation;
- receive analytics and statistics regarding the Profile.

## 7. Limitation of Platform Liability

7.1. The Platform is **NOT** a party to the legal relationship between the Attorney and the Client.

7.2. The Platform shall **NOT** be liable for:
- the content, quality, and results of the Attorney's legal consultations;
- decisions made by the Client based on consultations;
- actions or omissions of the Attorney;
- the Attorney's compliance with attorney ethics and applicable law;
- the Attorney's tax obligations.

7.3. The Platform provides technical infrastructure and AI tools "as is" and does not guarantee uninterrupted service operation.

7.4. The Platform's aggregate liability to the Attorney is limited to the amount of Commissions paid by the Attorney over the preceding 3 (three) months.

## 8. Non-Solicitation

8.1. The Attorney undertakes not to propose to Clients acquired through the Platform to continue cooperation outside the Platform in order to avoid paying the Commission.

8.2. The restriction set forth in Section 8.1 shall remain in effect for 12 (twelve) months from the date of the last Consultation with the respective Client conducted through the Platform.

8.3. In the event of violation of Section 8.1, the Attorney shall pay a penalty equal to the Commission that would have been payable for the relevant services, but no less than the sum of three average Commissions over the preceding 6 months.

8.4. The restriction in Section 8.1 does not apply to Clients with whom the Attorney had documented relationships prior to using the Platform.

9.3. The Platform may terminate the Agreement immediately in the cases specified in Section 4.2.

9.4. Termination of the Agreement does not release the parties from financial obligations arising prior to termination.
